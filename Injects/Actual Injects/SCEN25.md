## Purpose
This memo serves to document the blue team's response to Inject 24 SCEN25 incident Response Procedure. It will provide a clear record of any actions taken to ensure alignment with the operational objectives and goals of the stakeholders involved.

## Executive Summary
We were instructed to develop a Incident Response
Procedure for responding to future incidents in which an employee causes a malware infection, either due to web site browsing or acting upon spam email.

## Response
The blue team implemented the following measures:
1. Detection and Initial Reporting
   Automated Alerts, User Reporting, and Privileged Process Monitoring
2. Containment and Segmentation
   Immediate Isolation, Service Disabling, and Network Quarantine
3. Analysis and Scope Determination
   Impact Assessment, Lateral Movement Tracking, and  Persistence Investigation
4. Eradication and Remediation
   System Restoration, System Hardening, and Credential Rotation
5. Recovery and Post-Incident Activity
   Verification, Documentation, and Policy Review
## Conclusion
This is a first draft of the malware response procedure please let us know any edits you would like for us to make.
