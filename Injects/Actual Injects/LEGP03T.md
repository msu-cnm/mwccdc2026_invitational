## Purpose
This memo serves to document the blue team's response to Inject 19 LEGP03T Employee Browsing Policy. It will provide a clear record of any actions taken to ensure alignment with the operational objectives and goals of the stakeholders involved.

## Executive Summary
Since the organization has experienced disruption caused by employee browsing
leading to a vulnerability: an Employee Browsing policy is needed. We were tasked with creating a draft for one which is attached to this Memo.

## Response
1.) Purpose & Scope
	The primary purpose of this policy is to ensure a secure, productive, and legally compliant browsing environment. The goal of this policy is to minimize risk of malware, data breaches, and productivity loss caused by unauthorized or unsafe browsing habits. Scope: This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third parties accessing the internet via company-provided hardware, software, or network infrastructure
2.) A Statement on Acceptable Use
	Internet access is provided primarily for business-related tasks. While the company allows incidental and occasional personal use during breaks, such use must:
		Not interfere with work performance or network bandwidth.
		Adhere to all professional conduct standards.
		Never compromise the security of the corporate network.
3.) Specific Prohibited Uses
	Users are strictly prohibited from using company resources to access malicious sites, illegal activities, inappropriate content, or high-risk services
4.) Requirements
	To maintain full security posture, all users must adhere to the following:
		Only use Company Approved Browsers, maintained with regular updates
		Only use Company Approved Extensions
		DO NOT disable security features enabled by IT
5.) Expectations on Privacy and Monitoring
	While we respect individual privacy, employees should have no expectation of privacy when using company-owned equipment or networks. The organization reserves the right to monitor, log, and archive all internet traffic. Automated systems are in place to flag high-risk behavior or visits to blocked categories.
6.) Alignment with Legal Obligations
	This policy is designed to comply with data protection laws and industry-specific regulations. The company will cooperate with law enforcement if browsing logs are required for legal investigations regarding illegal online activity.
7.) Consequences for Non-Compliance
	Violations of this policy will be investigated and may result in:
	Revocation of internet privileges.
	Disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment.
	Legal action in cases of criminal conduct or significant financial loss to the company.
8.) Employee & Company Responsibilities
	Employee Responsibility: To browse mindfully, report suspicious pop-ups or "certificate errors" to IT immediately, and complete annual security awareness training. Company Responsibility: To provide secure tools, maintain updated web filters, and ensure monitoring is conducted fairly and solely for security and performance purposes.
9.) Employee Acknowledgement and Sign-off.
	I have read, understood, and agree to abide by the terms of this Employee Browsing Policy. I understand that internet access is a tool provided for business purposes and that my usage may be monitored for security compliance.
